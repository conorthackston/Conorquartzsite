# 👤 About Me

> [!abstract] **My Core Philosophy**
> I empower students to become **critical thinking digital citizens**. By integrating high-quality media literacy with cutting-edge technology, I create equitable, collaborative environments where students don't just survive the digital landscape—they thrive in it.

---

## 🏫 The Reality of the Classroom
> [!quote] Solving Teacher Burnout
> My passion for AI isn't just about the technology; it's about the **human element**. I’ve seen the burnout in our halls. I specialize in translating "scary" technical concepts into practical tools that save teachers time. I love the moment a skeptical educator realizes AI isn't a replacement, but a partner that handles the grunt work so they can get back to the heart of teaching.

## 🛡️ Digital Citizenship & AI Literacy
Coming from a **Library Media background**, I view AI through the lens of information literacy. Students shouldn't just *use* these tools; they must *understand* them. 

I have integrated specific curricula focused on:
- **Identifying Algorithmic Bias**
- **Navigating Misinformation**
- **Critical Thinking in an AI-Generated World**

## ⚖️ Curriculum & Equity
> [!important] AI is the ultimate lever for educational equity.
> My experience in Howard County has taught me that software isn't a solution—**strategy is.** I am passionate about moving districts past the "shiny toy" phase into deep integration that supports:
> - **Linguistically Diverse Learners**
> - **Personalized Instruction Paths**
> - **Scalable District Roadmaps**
